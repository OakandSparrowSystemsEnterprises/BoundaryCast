# BoundaryCast privacy policy draft
Publisher: Oak and Sparrow Systems Enterprise LLC. Contact: contact@oakandsparrowsystemsenterprise.io. Effective date to be set before release.

When you explicitly request a forecast, the Android app may ask for foreground approximate or precise location permission or use coordinates you enter. It sends coordinates rounded to four decimals directly to api.weather.gov using HTTPS to obtain weather data. NWS receives the request location and ordinary connection metadata, including IP address; its own privacy practices apply. Location is not used for advertising, an OASSE account, or background tracking. No OASSE service receives the request. The app does not save a location history, persistent forecast cache, account or analytics. The current view and transient receipt are in memory and cleared when leaving the app; no raw coordinates are included in the receipt. You can deny or revoke device location permission and use manual coordinates, clear the view, or uninstall the app. Opening the official weather website leaves the application. Google Play handles purchase and platform account data independently. Contact support with questions; avoid sending sensitive location information. This policy is a draft pending native device and store disclosure review.
