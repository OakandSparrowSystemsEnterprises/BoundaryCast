# BoundaryCast Play listing draft
Title: BoundaryCast: Personal Weather
Category: Weather
Package: io.oakandsparrow.boundarycast
Short description: Evidence-checked U.S. weather, station observations and official alerts.

Your weather, checked before it speaks. BoundaryCast compares available live National Weather Service forecast, station and alert evidence and shows the weather claim that the public policy supports. Read hourly area forecasts separately from nearby measured conditions, see when the evidence was updated, and review the decision reasons. If important evidence is unavailable, BoundaryCast withholds unsupported weather claims instead of displaying invented values.

Check your current location with foreground permission, or enter coordinates for a U.S. location. No account, ads, location history or proprietary backend. A connection to NWS is required. The app is independent and is not affiliated with NOAA or the National Weather Service. It is not an emergency-alert delivery service or a guarantee of exact-location weather or forecast accuracy. Follow official warnings and local emergency guidance.
