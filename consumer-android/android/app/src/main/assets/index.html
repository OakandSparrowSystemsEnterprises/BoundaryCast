About, privacy & safety

BoundaryCast is an independent app by Oak and Sparrow Systems Enterprise LLC. NWS data is attributed to the National Weather Service; no affiliation or endorsement is implied. Coverage is limited to locations served by NWS. This app does not replace NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts or local emergency instructions. No background monitoring or push alerts.

The app uses the location you provide only for an explicit foreground weather request. Coordinates are sent directly to api.weather.gov over HTTPS; the provider receives request metadata such as IP address and location. The app has no account, analytics, local weather history, advertising or OASSE server. Current results are held in memory and cleared on leaving the app. The official website opens in your browser, under its own privacy terms.

Policy is a public consumer adaptation of BoundaryCast's evidence and scope logic, not private OASSE math or Gatekeeper V2. Exact-location and microclimate claims are withheld in this edition. Receipts are transient policy replay records, not signed or independently verified proof.
